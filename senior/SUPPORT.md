# AddVenture Senior — Support

AddVenture Senior helps students in Years 11–13 practise choosing maths methods across 33 stations. It covers topics drawn from NCEA Levels 1–3. It is an independent learning game, not an official assessment guide or a replacement for a teacher or current course materials.

## Getting help

Email [nzbennycohen@gmail.com](mailto:nzbennycohen@gmail.com). Please tell us your Windows version, what you were doing, and what happened. For a suspected maths error, include the full question and worked steps if possible. Do not send a student’s personal details or saved working unless you choose to.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account or internet connection?** No account is needed. The Windows app works offline after installation.

**Where is progress saved?** On this device only. There is no cloud backup or sync. The grown-ups screen can show progress and saved handwritten working. Resetting progress removes it from the app.

**Can I change language?** Yes. Choose English, Spanish, German, Hebrew, or Simplified Chinese in the app. Changing language does not reset progress.

**Do I need the earlier AddVenture apps?** No. Senior is a separate app for Years 11–13; earlier apps are not required.

**Which curriculum does it follow?** Its maths topics are mapped to NCEA Levels 1–3. Course and assessment requirements can change, so check your school’s current guidance for exam preparation.
