# AddVenture Senior — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

AddVenture Senior is a maths methods game for students in Years 11–13 (approximately ages 15–18), published by Benny Cohen.

## The short version

The app does not collect personal information or send a student’s activity to us. It has no account, advertising, analytics, chat, or in-app purchases.

## Data on your device

The app stores the player name you choose, progress, answers, saved handwritten working, rewards, and settings in local storage on the device. This data stays on that device. It is not uploaded to us or to a cloud account. Parents or teachers can view saved progress and working in the app. A grown-up can choose to copy a progress summary to the device clipboard; the app does not send that copy to us. You can remove saved progress using the app’s reset option.

## Network and purchases

After installation, the Windows app works offline. It does not contact our servers. Microsoft handles Microsoft Store purchases, installation, and updates under Microsoft’s own privacy terms. We do not receive payment card details.

## Changes and contact

If a future version changes how data is used, we will update this policy before releasing it. Questions: [nzbennycohen@gmail.com](mailto:nzbennycohen@gmail.com).
