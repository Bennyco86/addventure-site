# AddVenture Secondary — Support

**AddVenture Secondary** is a maths strategy game for Years 9–10 (ages
13–15). It teaches the *tactic* that makes each problem easy — add the
exponents, cancel before you multiply, times 1.15 for GST, the four-box grid,
flip the sign for a negative — rather than drilling for speed. It follows
AddVenture (ages 8–11) and AddVenture Intermediate (Years 7–8), and is built on
the refreshed New Zealand mathematics curriculum for Years 9–10.

## Common questions

**Does my child need an account?**
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no password. Progress is saved on the
device itself.

**Does it need an internet connection?**
No. Once installed, AddVenture Secondary works entirely offline.

**Is my child's data safe?**
The app collects nothing and sends nothing. See the
[privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

**Where is my child's progress stored?**
On the device, in local storage. It is not backed up to any server. Uninstalling
the app, clearing site data, or using the reset option inside the app will erase
it.

**Can I see how my child is doing?**
Yes — the "For grown-ups" screen shows every technique in each of the seven
sections and whether your child has genuinely secured it. A technique only
counts as secure once they have chosen it themselves and then applied it to a
fresh problem unprompted, so getting answers right by luck does not register.
Saved handwritten working can be reviewed there too.

**Do I need the earlier AddVenture apps?**
No. AddVenture Secondary starts at Year 9 and teaches everything it uses.
The apps share a style, not progress.

**Which curriculum does it follow?**
The New Zealand Years 9–10 sequence (Number, Algebra and Measurement). Grown-ups
can switch the labels to age-based ones for anywhere else; the maths is the
same.

**Can I change the language?**
Yes — English, Español, Deutsch, 简体中文 and עברית, from the title screen or
the grown-ups screen. Changing language never resets progress.

**My child got an answer marked wrong that looks right.**
Fractions and decimals are accepted in any equivalent form (9/2, 18/4 and 4.5
all match 4.5) unless the question is specifically about the form of the answer.
If you believe a question or its working is genuinely wrong, email the exact
question text — every one is checked automatically, and a real mistake will be
fixed in the next update.
