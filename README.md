# AddVenture

**A mental maths strategy adventure for children aged 8–11.**

Most maths apps drill for speed. AddVenture teaches the *tricks* instead — the
mental strategies that make a problem easy before you calculate anything. Faced
with 19 + 17, a child learns to spot "make 20": move 1 across and it becomes
20 + 16.

Every strategy is a card they unlock. They see *why* it works with a proper
visual proof, then apply it to a fresh problem to show they have understood it.
Nothing is timed, and counting on your fingers still works — it is just the slow
road.

Covers addition and subtraction strategies, times-table tricks, division with
remainders, fractions, decimals, area and perimeter, and the divisibility rules.
A harder setting adds volume and percentages on top, when a grown-up decides
they are ready.

- No advertising
- No accounts, no sign-up
- No data collection of any kind
- Works completely offline
- A parent dashboard showing which techniques a child genuinely understands
- Optional real-world rewards, set by the parent, earned by mastery — never by
  time spent on screen
